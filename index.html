1. Field & Flour 面包店 Logo（SVG） / Field & Flour bakery logo (SVG)

SVG Logo · awesome case #6 · output_format=svg, n=1 · source

awesome #6

EN（官方/文档原文）

Create an original, non-infringing logo for a company called Field & Flour, a local bakery.
The logo should feel warm, simple, and timeless. Use clean, vector-like shapes, a strong silhouette, and balanced negative space.
Favor simplicity over detail so it reads clearly at small and large sizes. Flat design, minimal strokes, no gradients unless essential.
Fully transparent background. Deliver a single centered logo with generous padding, clean alpha edges, and no solid backdrop, scenery, checkerboard, or watermark.

ZH（同义复述，便于复用）

为一家本地面包店「Field & Flour」创建一个原创、不侵权的 Logo。
Logo 应温暖、简洁、耐看。使用干净的矢量感形状、清晰剪影、平衡留白。
偏简洁而非细节，保证小尺寸与大尺寸都清晰可读。平面设计、最少描边，非必要不用渐变。
背景完全透明。输出单个居中 Logo，留足边距，干净的透明边缘；不要实心底、场景、棋盘格或水印。

2. 提灯小猴（循环 GIF） / Monkey with lantern (looping GIF)

循环 GIF · awesome case #2 · output_format=gif, size=512x512, quality=high, frame_count=9, frame_duration_ms=200 · source

awesome #2

ZH（同义复述，便于复用）

猴子把一只琥珀色发光纸灯笼举在胸前，慢慢抬起、抬头眨眼，再放回起始姿势。
灯笼始终在手里，相机固定。动作要能平滑循环。

3. 渔民与狗（Flare vs Sunburst） / Elderly sailor & dog (Flare vs Sunburst)

纪实摄影 · awesome case #3 · Flare + Sunburst comparison · source

awesome #3

Create a photorealistic candid photograph of an elderly sailor standing on a small fishing boat.

皮肤有可见皱纹、毛孔与日晒质感，手臂有几处褪色的传统水手纹身。
他正平静地整理渔网，狗坐在甲板附近。35mm 胶片感，中近景平视，约 50mm。
柔和海岸日光、浅景深、轻微胶片颗粒、自然色彩。画面要诚实、不摆拍，保留真实皮肤与旧物细节。不要过度美化或重度修图。

7. 四格漫画：宠物在家狂欢 / 4-panel pet party comic

漫画 · awesome case #7 · Sunburst · source

awesome #7

EN（官方/文档原文）

Create a short vertical comic-style reel with 4 panels.
Panel 1: The owner leaves through the front door. The pet is framed in the window behind them, small against the glass, eyes wide, paws pressed high, the house suddenly quiet.
Panel 2: The door clicks shut. Silence breaks. The pet slowly turns toward the empty house, posture shifting, eyes sharp with possibility.
Panel 3: The house transformed. The pet sprawls across the couch like it owns the place, crumbs nearby, sunlight cutting across the room like a spotlight.
Panel 4: The door opens. The pet is seated perfectly by the entrance, alert and composed, as if nothing happened.

ZH（同义复述，便于复用）

做一条竖版四格漫画风短片。
第 1 格：主人从前门离开，宠物在身后窗户里，身子小、眼睛睁大、爪子按在玻璃上，屋子突然安静。
第 2 格：门咔哒关上。安静被打破。宠物慢慢转向空屋，姿态变化，眼里带着可能性。
第 3 格：屋子变了样。宠物摊在沙发上像主人，旁边有碎屑，阳光像聚光灯切开房间。
第 4 格：门开了。宠物端坐门口，警觉又镇定，仿佛什么都没发生。

9. 细胞呼吸一览图 / Cellular respiration diagram

科学图 · awesome case #9 · Sunburst · source

awesome #9

EN（官方/文档原文）

Create a simple biology diagram titled "Cellular Respiration at a Glance" for high school students.

Show how glucose turns into energy inside a cell. Include glycolysis, the Krebs cycle, and the electron transport chain.
Use arrows to connect the steps, and label the main molecules: glucose, pyruvate, ATP, NADH, FADH2, CO2, O2, and H2O.
Make it look like a clean classroom handout or slide, with a white background, simple icons, clear labels, and easy-to-read text.

Avoid tiny text, extra decoration, or anything that makes the diagram hard to understand.

ZH（同义复述，便于复用）

为高中生做一张简洁生物图，标题为「Cellular Respiration at a Glance」。
展示葡萄糖在细胞内如何变成能量，包含糖酵解、Krebs 循环与电子传递链。
用箭头连接步骤，标注主要分子：glucose、pyruvate、ATP、NADH、FADH2、CO2、O2、H2O。
像干净讲义或课件：白底、简单图标、清晰标签、易读文字。避免过小文字、多余装饰或难懂排版。

贺卡 · awesome case #13 · Sunburst; exact card text · source

awesome #13

EN（官方/文档原文）

Create a Christmas holiday card illustration.

Scene:
a cozy Christmas scene with an old teddy bear sitting inside a keepsake box, slightly worn fur, soft stitching repairs, placed near a window with falling snow outside. The scene suggests the child has grown up, but the memories remain.

Mood:
Warm, nostalgic, gentle, emotional.

Style:
Premium holiday card photography, soft cinematic lighting,
realistic textures, shallow depth of field,
tasteful bokeh lights, high print-quality composition.

Constraints:

- No trademarks
- No watermarks
- No logos

"Merry Christmas — some memories never fade."

创作一张圣诞贺卡插画。
